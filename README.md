# 🏛️ Majhi Mati
## Phase-wise Development Monitoring & Governance System

> **माझं शहर • माझी जबाबदारी • माझा विकास**

Majhi Mati is a digital development-monitoring and governance platform designed to create **transparent, accountable, citizen-centric and phase-wise monitoring of government projects**.

The platform connects **Citizens, Municipal Officers and Government Project Data** through a single digital system.

It enables authorized officers to manage development projects while allowing citizens to view project information, progress, milestones, budgets, documents, issues and feedback.

---

## 🎯 Vision

Government development projects often involve multiple stages, departments, approvals, contractors, financial transactions and inspections.

Majhi Mati brings these activities into one structured digital workflow.

The objective is to provide:

- 🔎 Project transparency
- 📊 Phase-wise development monitoring
- 👥 Citizen participation
- 🏛️ Officer governance
- 💰 Budget transparency
- 📄 Document visibility
- 📈 Progress tracking
- ⚠️ Issue & risk monitoring
- 📝 Citizen feedback
- 🧾 Audit trail
- 📊 KPI-based decision making
